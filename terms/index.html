Your use of that provider is governed by your agreement with it, not by us.

Check what the AI produces

AI output can be wrong in ways that are hard to spot: a mistranslated term, a changed number, a sentence left out or added, a misplaced tag, or a text that reads well but means something else. The software's checks help, but they do not catch everything. Review AI output before you deliver it, file it or rely on it. This matters most where an error has consequences – legal, medical, financial or patent texts, for example. The translation you deliver is your work and your responsibility, however it was produced.

Confidentiality

Using a cloud AI provider sends the text you work on to that provider. Before you do, make sure your clients' instructions, your confidentiality agreements and the law allow it – including data protection law, if the text contains personal data. If they do not, use a model on your own computer, or do that work without AI. We never receive the text: see the privacy policy.

AI costs

Your AI provider bills you directly for what you use. Cost figures shown in the software are estimates; your provider's bill is what counts, and it can be higher. We recommend setting a spending limit with your provider.

AI assistants

If you connect an AI assistant, such as Claude Desktop or ChatGPT, to the Supervertaler MCP Server, it can read your open projects and, when you ask it to, change segments, comments, termbases, translation memories and memory banks. It acts on your instructions, under its provider's terms. Review what it changes.
